Product Suite

Two Products.
One System.

The Aerovyn system is purpose-built for drone-based payload delivery in time-critical environments. It is designed to work with platforms already in the field, requiring no new airframe procurement and no changes to existing drone operations.

01 / Primary Payload
Aerovyn Capsule
AC-1 · Payload System

A thermally managed medical supply payload designed for drone delivery in contested, remote, or access-denied environments. Built to address the leading causes of preventable traumatic death. Engineered for rapid, one-handed deployment by personnel under stress.

Active Thermal TCCC / TECC Single-Use Blue UAS
02 / Interface Hardware
Recovery Mount
ARM-1 · Bracket System

A reusable mechanical interface system that attaches the payload to compatible drone platforms. Designed for reliable deployment and drone recovery after every mission, with no modifications to existing airframe hardware required.

Reusable Universally Mountable Passive Release
Revenue Model
Razor + Blade

The ARM-1 bracket is a one-time acquisition per drone. Each mission consumes one AC-1 capsule. Agencies that equip their fleet create a predictable recurring resupply requirement on installed hardware.

Target Markets

Who We Serve

Aerovyn is built for operators already running drone programs who need a medical resupply capability without procuring new hardware.

Defense

Squad-level medical resupply for military units operating in contested environments where ground-based casualty evacuation is delayed or not possible.

Law Enforcement

Tactical law enforcement agencies running drone programs who need a medical resupply capability without adding new platforms or procurement cycles.

Fire / USAR

Urban Search and Rescue and fire departments responding to mass casualty and structural collapse scenarios where personnel access is limited.

Industrial Safety

Energy, mining, and pipeline operators with remote workforces beyond practical EMS response range where drone fleets are already deployed.
